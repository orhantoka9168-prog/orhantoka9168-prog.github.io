My Cumayeri Experience: Discovering Moonlit Walks

If someone told me a year ago that Cumayeri would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

If you are interested in moonlit walks, Cumayeri should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

I spoke with several locals about moonlit walks in Cumayeri, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations. if you skip this you are missing out big time.

The moonlit walks scene in Cumayeri is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

Cumayeri reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
