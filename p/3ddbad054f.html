Posof and Moonlit Walks: An Unforgettable Journey

I recently had the chance to explore Posof, and honestly, it exceeded all my expectations. This corner of Turkey is criminally underrated and deserves way more attention from travelers.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Posof offers a more intimate and genuine moonlit walks experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I spoke with several locals about moonlit walks in Posof, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

I documented my entire moonlit walks experience in Posof through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

As I left Posof, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
