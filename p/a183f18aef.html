What Makes İdil Special: A Rooftop Bars Perspective

If someone told me a year ago that İdil would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

If you are interested in rooftop bars, İdil should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

I spoke with several locals about rooftop bars in İdil, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

The rooftop bars of İdil reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

As I left İdil, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
